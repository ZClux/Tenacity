Creamy Keys - recorded keyboard sounds
======================================

All clips were converted to mono 16-bit 44.1 kHz WAV, trimmed and peak-normalised.

1) kbsim by Thomas Lai - https://github.com/tplai/kbsim (src/assets/audio)
   Licence: MIT (Copyright (c) Thomas Lai)
   Profiles: nkcream (cream), holypanda, alpaca, topre, turquoise, boxnavy, mxblue, mxbrown, mxblack,
             bluealps, buckling, blackink, redink

2) Mechvibes by hainguyents13 - https://github.com/hainguyents13/mechvibes (soundpacks/keyboard)
   Licence: MIT (Copyright (c) Hai Nguyen)
   Profiles: oreo (eg-oreo), mxblackpbt (cherrymx-black-pbt) - split from the single-file sprite using the
             config.json "definitions" timings.
